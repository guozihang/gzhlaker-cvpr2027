\subsection{Data, metric and protocol}
\label{sec:exp-data}
The audited local VCSL release~\cite{vcsl2022} has 12,254 fixed ISC feature files and 97,033
training, 41,798 validation and 54,630 test pairs. Validation is positive-only; 27,315 test
pairs lack a GT key and count as negatives, and coordinates are half-open feature indices.
Structural settings are chosen on \texttt{dev\_design}, thresholds on
\texttt{dev\_calibration}, and the audit is held out in \texttt{dev\_audit}, grouped by shared
video across splits; validation cannot calibrate test FAR, which stays held out. The target
is about 200 real pairs covering short, edited, repeated, similar and multi-segment copies,
plus at least 300 anchor and 150 fusion-edge decisions; if fewer exist, the count is reported
as-is. Action labels require human review or traceable transform truth, otherwise the actions
stay in shadow.

For predictions $P_k$ and GT boxes $G_l$, let $O_k^A$ be the union of A-axis projections of
their positive-area intersections and $O_k^B$ the analogue; $Q_l^A,Q_l^B$ reverse the roles.
The evaluator computes
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
P_p&=\frac{\sum_k\ell(O_k^A)}{\sum_k\ell(P_{k,A})+\epsilon}
     \frac{\sum_k\ell(O_k^B)}{\sum_k\ell(P_{k,B})+\epsilon},\\
R_p&=\frac{\sum_l\ell(Q_l^A)}{\sum_l\ell(G_{l,A})+\epsilon}
     \frac{\sum_l\ell(Q_l^B)}{\sum_l\ell(G_{l,B})+\epsilon},
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:metric}
\end{equation}
and macro averages whose exact denominators are in the supplement, with $\ell$ interval
length and $\epsilon=10^{-6}$. Products of axis coverage replace min-IoU or area IoU. Empty
prediction against nonempty GT gives $(1,0)$, the reverse $(0,1)$, both empty $(1,1)$, and
exactly-one values are excluded. Report \texttt{n\_expected}, \texttt{n\_processed}
(\textsc{ok}+\textsc{empty}), both denominators, failures, skips and exclusions; an empty
denominator is undefined and an exception is never an empty prediction.

\subsection{Baselines and controlled comparisons}
\label{sec:exp-baselines}
Classical HV/TN/DP/DTW and compatible pretrained SPD/TransVCL are planned reruns under the
same inputs and evaluator; checkpoints and runnable environments are prerequisites. E1 uses
one zero-phase grid, mutual Top-$K_{\rm nn}$, RANSAC, fixed-width bands, one path per band,
and shared scoring and post-processing; it omits gap splitting and the extra dual-axis checks
but keeps the recurrence, margin and support rule. Only E3 freezes identical tubes. E0, E2
and E4 compare external baselines, stride/scale/phase changes and single structural factors
against executed dots; E5 compares scan and direct offset after semantic parity. Core
consistency and E1/E3 precede shadow decisions, action controls, the joint four-cell
comparison, and then the quality--cost curve.

\subsection{Risk, decisions and timing}
\label{sec:exp-risk}
We freeze five quantities: true anchor deletion over audited true anchors, reported over all
$\Anch_0$ and over queried true anchors separately; deletion action error (wrongly deleted
true anchors over all deletions); wrong-fusion rate (wrongly accepted merges over all
accepted merges, with opportunities and missed merges); coverage and abstention counts; and
final P/R/F1/FAR with paired recall difference and boundary MAE on a common-GT cohort.
Targets are $1\%$ deletion, $1\%$ wrong fusion and a $0.5$-point recall drop, not
certificates. For $n$ independent zero-error trials the one-sided $95\%$ bound is
$1-0.05^{1/n}$ ($1.98\%$ at $n=150$, $0.994\%$ at $n=300$), so 299 effective trials are
needed for $1\%$; correlated observations use predeclared grouped intervals (supplement).

Each E6 row fixes the correct upstream input. E6-A compares no action, random selection, a
margin/geometry rule and time-spread selection from the same $\Anch_0$. E6-F compares no
fusion, geometry plus connection only, random visual and selective visual from the same
$\mathcal C_0$ and edge pool, reporting legal-edge coverage, $\mathrm{Conn}$ pass rate and
asked-versus-executed actions. E6-J reruns core, A only, F only and A+F without adding gains;
its four cells fix A8, F4 and A8+F4, while A12/F12/A8+F4 states whether transfer is enabled.
Neither is an equal-frame or equal-token control. E6-S/R/V test shared execution, readout
separation and visual necessity (supplement).

$\Tloc$ starts with features resident and initialized operators and ends with in-memory
predictions; $\Taug$ adds decode, evidence, model calls, retries, connection DP and output.
We warm up, repeat at least three rounds, fix threads, separate cold cache, first-call and
steady state, and never compare a warm-cache number with a cold baseline; the full protocol
is in the supplement.

\paragraph{Evidence limits.}
\label{sec:exp-status}
All result rows are pending. The near-complete validation execution scored 41,797 of 41,798
pairs with one error under an unfrozen configuration, and \texttt{dev1000} covers 200 pairs.
No visual backend, verified frame map or action threshold is validated, and the snapshot has
no source video, so the decision layer is interface-level only. Synthetic checks establish
semantics, not accuracy; no benchmark claim is admissible until a frozen protocol and
full-set risk and cost artifacts exist.
