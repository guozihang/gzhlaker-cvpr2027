\subsection{Data and selection protocol}
\label{sec:exp-data}
The audited local VCSL release~\cite{vcsl2022} contains 12,254 fixed ISC feature files,
97,033 training pairs, 41,798 validation pairs and 54,630 test pairs. Validation is
positive-only; the test list contains 27,315 GT-key-missing pairs, treated as negatives by
the local loader. Coordinates are half-open feature indices, not seconds or source frames.
We preserve original GT and separately count degenerate or out-of-range annotations.

Choose structural settings on development data and each threshold on validation with a
predeclared grid, objective and tie rule; freeze before test. Validation is selection-biased
and has no negatives, so it cannot calibrate test FAR; without a provenance-audited negative
set, FAR stays held out. Audit shared videos across splits before treating pairs as
independent. Development data are grouped by shared videos into \texttt{dev\_design}
(structure and selector), \texttt{dev\_calibration} (action thresholds) and \texttt{dev\_audit}
(held-out check). The first target is about 200 real pairs covering short copies, strong
edits, repeated shots, semantically similar non-copies and multi-segment copies, plus at
least 300 anchor and 150 fusion-edge decisions. If fewer exist, the count is reported as-is;
synthetic samples do not fill the gap. Action labels require human review or traceable
transform truth; without reliable labels the actions stay in shadow.

\subsection{Metric and completion accounting}
\label{sec:exp-metric}
For predictions $P_k$ and GT boxes $G_l$, let $O_k^A$ be the union of A-axis projections of
their positive-area intersections and $O_k^B$ the analogue; $Q_l^A,Q_l^B$ reverse the roles.
The reference evaluator computes
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
P_p&=\frac{\sum_k\ell(O_k^A)}{\sum_k\ell(P_{k,A})+\epsilon}
     \frac{\sum_k\ell(O_k^B)}{\sum_k\ell(P_{k,B})+\epsilon},\\
R_p&=\frac{\sum_l\ell(Q_l^A)}{\sum_l\ell(G_{l,A})+\epsilon}
     \frac{\sum_l\ell(Q_l^B)}{\sum_l\ell(G_{l,B})+\epsilon},
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:metric}
\end{equation}
with $\ell$ interval length and $\epsilon=10^{-6}$: products of axis coverage, not min-IoU or
area IoU. Empty predictions return $(P_p,R_p)=(1,0)$ against nonempty GT, nonempty
predictions $(0,1)$ against empty GT, and both empty $(1,1)$; exactly-one values are excluded
from each mean, and
\begin{equation}
\bar P=\frac{\sum_{p\in\mathcal D_P}P_p}{|\mathcal D_P|},\quad
\bar R=\frac{\sum_{p\in\mathcal D_R}R_p}{|\mathcal D_R|},\quad
\mathrm{F1}=\frac{2\bar P\bar R}{\bar P+\bar R+\epsilon},
\label{eq:macro}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal D_P=\{p:P_p\ne1\}$ and $\mathcal D_R=\{p:R_p\ne1\}$. Report
\texttt{n\_expected}, \texttt{n\_processed} (\textsc{ok}+\textsc{empty}), both macro
denominators, failures, skips and equality exclusions; an empty denominator is undefined,
not zero, and an exception is never an empty prediction. Candidate-recall and boundary-MAE
diagnostics, including matching and cohort rules, are defined in the supplement.

\subsection{Baselines and controlled experiments}
\label{sec:exp-baselines}
Classical HV/TN/DP/DTW and compatible pretrained SPD/TransVCL are planned reruns under the
same inputs and evaluator. A checkpoint, feature interface and runnable environment are
prerequisites, not established facts. The simple sparse-to-band baseline (E1) uses one
zero-phase grid, mutual Top-$K_{\rm nn}$, RANSAC, fixed-width bands, one path per band, and
shared scoring, threshold selection and min-IoU post-processing; it omits gap splitting and
the extra dual-axis support/span checks, but must use the same recurrence, margin and support
rule as the core it is compared with. Only E3 freezes identical tubes.

E0 compares external baselines; E2 varies stride, nested scales and independent phases against
executed dots; E3 holds tracks and tubes and compares support boxes, band DTW, prototype
continuation and the zero-floor recurrence; E4 varies one structural factor at a time; E5
compares scan and direct-offset after semantic parity, with full-matrix DP as a separate
search-domain comparison. Five seeds measure fit variability, not data significance. Core
consistency and E1/E3 run first, then shadow decisions, action controls, the joint four-cell
comparison, and only then the full quality--cost curve and external baselines.

\subsection{Action risk and audit}
\label{sec:exp-risk}
We freeze five quantities before choosing sample size. \emph{True anchor deletion rate:}
deleted true-copy anchors over audited true-copy anchors, reported over all $\Anch_0$ and
over queried true anchors, so unqueried items do not dilute risk. \emph{Deletion action
error:} wrongly deleted true anchors over all deletion actions, \texttt{N/A} when there are
none. \emph{Wrong-fusion rate:} wrongly accepted merges over all accepted merges, with correct
opportunities among legal edges and missed merges also reported. \emph{Coverage and
abstention:} asked, verified, acted, abstained or failed, counted separately. \emph{Final
effect:} full P/R/F1/FAR, paired recall difference with uncertainty, and boundary MAE on a
common-GT cohort. Targets are a $1\%$ true-anchor deletion rate, a $1\%$ wrong-fusion rate and
a $0.5$ percentage-point recall drop; they are operating targets, not certificates.

For $n$ independent zero-error trials the one-sided $95\%$ bound is
$p_{\rm upper}=1-0.05^{1/n}$: $1.98\%$ at $n=150$ and $0.994\%$ at $n=300$, so at least 299
effective trials are needed for a bound no larger than $1\%$. Candidate edges usually do not
yield as many accepted fusions; video-correlated observations violate independence and
grouped intervals are predeclared. Thresholds are selected on \texttt{dev\_calibration} and
frozen to \texttt{dev\_audit}; the supplement gives the full risk-ledger template and
grouped-interval rules.

\subsection{Two decision points (E6)}
\label{sec:exp-e6}
Each E6 row fixes the correct upstream input. \textbf{E6-A} compares no action, random
selection, a margin/geometry rule and time-spread selection from the same $\Anch_0$ and input
cap. \textbf{E6-F} compares no fusion, geometry plus connection only, random visual and
selective visual from the same $\mathcal C_0$ and legal edge pool, and reports correct
opportunities, legal-edge coverage, $\mathrm{Conn}$ pass rate and asked-versus-executed
actions on a fixed edge set. \textbf{E6-J} reruns core, A only, F only and A+F and never adds
gains across rows. \textbf{E6-S}, \textbf{E6-R} and \textbf{E6-V} test shared execution,
readout/post-training separation and visual necessity respectively; their full matrix is in
the supplement. A cheap visual or local-correspondence control on the same frames rules out a
gain from seeing more pixels. Core consistency and E1/E3 precede these rows.

\subsection{Timing and quality--cost analysis}
\label{sec:exp-e5}
$\Tloc$ starts with raw features resident in CPU memory and initialized operators, and ends
with in-memory predictions. Include normalization, transfers, matching, RANSAC, DP, traceback
and post-processing; separate feature I/O, export and initialization, and measure every
TransVCL chunk and assembly. Disable intrusive profiling, warm up, repeat at least three
rounds, fix threads and synchronize GPU endpoints. For the augmented system, $\Taug$ starts
before frame reading and includes decode, evidence assembly, geometry pre-fit, transfer,
model calls, retries, connection DP, score reconstruction and output; cold cache,
first-call initialisation and steady state are separate rows. A warm-cache number is not
compared with a cold-cache baseline, routing time does not stand in for model time, and score
thresholds are not used to fake an upstream saving.
